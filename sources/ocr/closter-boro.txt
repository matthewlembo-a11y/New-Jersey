BOROUGH OF CLOSTER
RESOLUTION #2025-180
JUNE 26, 2025
ENDORSING THE 2025 HOUSING ELEMENT
AND FAIR SHARE PLAN
WHEREAS, the Borough of Closter, County of Bergen, State of New Jersey, (hereinafter, “Closter”) has a
demonstrated history of voluntary compliance with its constitutional affordable housing obligations; and
WHEREAS, on March 20, 2024, Governor Murphy signed into law an Amendment to the 1985 Fair Housing Act,
N.J.S.A. 52:27D-301 et seq. (hereinafter “Amended FHA” or the “Act”); and
WHEREAS, among other things, the Act abolished the Council on Affordable Housing (hereinafter, “COAH”),
and replaced it with seven retired, on recall judges designated as the Program and authorized the Director of the
Administrative Office of the Courts, (hereinafter, respectively, “Director” and “AOC”) to create a framework to
process applications for affordable housing compliance certification; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to the Act and to Directive #14-24 issued by the Director on December
13, 2024, a
municipality desiring to participate in the Program was obligated to take the following actions by January 31, 2025:
(1) adopt a “binding resolution” determining its present and prospective need affordable housing obligations and (2)
file an action in the form of a declaratory judgment complaint in the County in which the municipality is located
within 48 hours after the municipality’s adoption of a binding resolution; and
WHEREAS, Closter adopted
a binding resolution on
its present and
prospective need affordable housing
obligations and filed the required declaratory judgment action in a timely manner; and
AX
WHEREAS, the Act also requires that a Housing Element and Fair Share Plan (hereinafter, “HEFSP”) be prepared
hus
and adopted by the Closter Planning Board (hereinafter, “Planning Board”), and endorsed by the Closter Governing
8
Body (hereinafter, “Governing Body”), by June 30, 2025; and
WHEREAS, the Planning Board adopted the “Housing Plan Element and Fair Share Plan, Round 4” for the
Borough of Closter, Bergen County, New Jersey, prepared by Caroline Z. Reiter, P.P., AICP of T&M Associates
dated (hereinafter referred to as“2025 HEFSP”), as an amendment to Closter’s Master Plan on June 26, 2025; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body desires to endorse the 2025 HEFSP adopted by the Planning Board on June 26,
2025.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Mayor and Council of the Borough of Closter:
1.
The Governing Body does hereby endorse the 2025 HEFSP adopted by the Planning Board on June 26,
2025.
2.
The Governing Body does hereby authorize the Borough Attorney to file this Resolution endorsing the
2025 HEFSP adopted by the Planning Board on eCourts for review by the Program.
3.
The Mayor and Clerk, together with other appropriate officers and employees of Closter are hereby
authorized to take all steps necessary to effectuate the purposes of this Resolution.
4.
This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

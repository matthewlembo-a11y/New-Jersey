BOROUGH OF MIDLAND PARK
COUNTY OF BERGEN
PLANNING BOARD
MEMORIALIZING RESOLUTION
OFFERED BY: Youricil Man Mark BOLUS
SECONDED BY: Stednanié tantale
Ata special and public meeting of the Midland Park Planning held on Monday, March 9,
2026
WHEREAS, the Planning Board of the Borough of Midland Park adopted a Third Round
Housing Element and Fair Share Plan as part of its Master Plan on May 21, 2018 (“2018
HEFSP”); and
WHEREAS, the Planning Board reviewed a new Housing Element and Fair Share Plan
prepared by Burgis Associates, Inc. dated May 30, 2025 (“2025 HEFSP”) to address the
Borough’s Fourth Round affordable housing obligations; and
WHEREAS, upon proper notice, the Planning Board of the Borough of Midland Park held a
public hearing on June 16, 2025, concerning the 2025 HEFSP which included testimony of
its planner, John Szabo, PP; and
WHEREAS, the Planning Board carefully considered any and all comments made by
members of the public at said hearing; and
WHEREAS, the Planning Board determined that the 2025 HEFSP addressed the Borough’s
Fair Share obligation and its capacity to accommodate the present and prospective
affordable housing needs in accordance with proper land use planning; and
WHEREAS, the Planning Board adopted the 2025 HEFSP on June 16, 2025, and the Master
Plan of the Borough of Midland Park was amended by replacing the 2018 HEFSP with the
2025 HEFSP; and
WHEREAS, following the adoption of the 2025 HEFSP, Fair Share Housing Center (“FSHC”)
filed a challenge pursuant to N.J.S.A. 52:27D-304.1(f)(2)(b) regarding the 2025 HEFSPon
September 2, 2025; and

WHEREAS, representatives of the Borough and FSHC entered into a Mediation Agreement
dated December 18, 2025, that resolved the issues raised in the challenge subject to, among
other requirements, the adoption of an Amended Housing Element and Fair Share Plan
consistent with the Mediation Agreement; and
WHEREAS, Burgis Associates, Inc. prepared an Amended Housing Element and Fair Share
Plan, dated January 9, 2026 (2026 Amended HEFSP), consistent with and reflecting the
Mediation Agreement, and further addressing the manner in which the Borough of Midland
Park will satisfy its affordable housing obligation; and
WHEREAS, upon proper notice, the Planning Board of the Borough of Midland Park held a
public hearing on this date (March 9, 2026) concerning the 2026 Amended HEFSP, which
included testimony of its planner, David Novack, PP; and
WHEREAS, the Planning Board reviewed and carefully considered any and all comments
made by members of the public at said hearing; and
WHEREAS, the Planning Board has determined that the 2026 Amended HEFSP attached
hereto, has addressed the Borough’s Fair Share obligation and its capacity to accommodate
the present and prospective affordable housing needs in accordance with proper land use
planning.
NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved that the Planning Board hereby adopts the 2026 Amended
HEFSP and the Master Plan of the Borough of Midland Park is hereby amended by replacing
the 2025 HEFSP with the 2026 Amended HEFSP.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that a copy of this Resolution andthe 2026 Amened HEFSP shall
be filed promptly with the Bergen County Planning Board and the Office of Planning
Advocacy.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that a copy of this Resolution and the 2026 Amended HEFSP be
sent to the municipal clerk of each adjoining municipality who requests a copy of same.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Planning Board Secretary shall transmit a certified,
electronic copy of the 2026 Amended HEFSP and this Resolution to the Borough’s Affordable
Housing Counsel
for further disposition.

AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND THE LAND USE AND ZONING ORDINANCE, TO CREATE AN
AFFORDABLE HOUSING DISTRICT IN CONNECTION WITH THE BOROUGH'S
AFFORDABLE HOUSING OBLIGATION
WHEREAS, the Borough of Oakland has previously adopted certain ordinances related to the
Borough’s affordable housing obligation; and
WHEREAS, the Borough is considering the adoption of its Fourth Round Housing Plan and Fair Share
Element related to the Borough’s affordable housing obligation.
NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED by the Mayor and Council of Oakland as follows:
SECTION 1. Chapter 59, Article VII, Zoning; Map, Section 59-46, Zoning districts established; Zoning
Map; interpretation of boundaries, is amended and supplemented to include the following zoning
district:
RA-6AH
Affordable Housing
SECTION 2. Chapter 59, Article VII, Zoning, is supplemented to establish a new Section 59-52.9, RA-
6AH Affordable Housing Zone, to read as follows:
A.
Purpose. Oakland is constitutionally obligated to provide a reasonable opportunity to comply
with its affordable housing obligation. Establishing the RA-6AH Zone will assist the Borough is
satisfying this obligation by creating a reasonable opportunity for the construction of
affordable housing. The purpose of the RA-6AH Zone is to permit development of a tract now
or formerly designated as Block 3301, Lot 2, and Block 3401, Lots 11, 12, 19, 40-43, 52, 53, 57,
and 58, as an inclusionary residential development consisting of not more than 240 residential
units, with a minimum of 20 percent of permitted and delivered units being affordable housing
units. This section recognizes that much of the land within the RA-6AH Zone is wooded and
that many of the slopes in the RA-6AH Zone exceed 15 percent. The standards in this section
shall supersede any conflicting standards in the Borough Code to the extent specifically
identified herein.
B.
Definitions. The following definitions are applicable to the RA-6AH Zone:
1.
DWELLING, STACKED TOWNHOUSE: An attached dwelling containing a minimum of
two dwelling units and a maximum of three dwelling units, each with separate outside
access and separated by either vertical and/or horizontal construction.
2.
TRACT: For purposes of this section, the word or phrase “Tract” or “Tract Lot Line”
shall be synonymous with the RA-6AH Zone itself. Since the intention of the Tract
development is to be planned as an integrated whole, the bulk requirements as noted
in the RA-6AH district are to apply to either the Tract or internal lot or lot line as
identified herein.
3.
Refer to §59-44 for additional applicable definitions.
4,
Interpretation. To the extent an existing Land Use and Zoning Ordinance definition or
